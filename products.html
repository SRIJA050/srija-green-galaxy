BIOPOT COLLECTION

Grow Plants.
Not Plastic.

Our Biopots are created by transforming groundnut shells, an agricultural residue, into useful planting products.

They provide a sustainable alternative to conventional plastic nursery bags while giving agricultural waste a productive second life.

GROUNDNUT SHELLS BIODEGRADABLE SUSTAINABLE
02

FROM WASTE TO VALUE

An Agricultural Waste,
Reimagined.

Groundnut shells are collected and processed to create useful, value-added products instead of leaving this agricultural residue underutilized.

Through our production process, these shells become part of a sustainable planting solution that gives agricultural waste a productive second life.

WASTE VALORIZATION VALUE CREATION INNOVATION
03

ECO-FRIENDLY VINAYAKA

Celebrate
With Nature.

Our eco-friendly Vinayaka idols are created using agricultural-waste-based materials, bringing sustainability into traditional celebrations.

The idea is simple — celebrate with devotion while being mindful of the materials we return to the environment after the festival.

NATURAL MATERIALS ECO-CONSCIOUS RESPONSIBLE CELEBRATION
04

AFTER THE CELEBRATION

Back to
Nature.

Our eco-friendly approach continues beyond the celebration. The use of biodegradable, natural materials is intended to reduce persistent synthetic waste after immersion.

By choosing environmentally conscious materials, celebrations can be connected more thoughtfully with water, nature and aquatic ecosystems.
