OUR INNOVATION

Groundnut Shell Biopots

Our biodegradable Biopots are a sustainable alternative to plastic nursery bags and pots. Made from natural agro-waste materials, these biopots are designed to support healthy plant growth while protecting the soil and environment.

They can be planted directly into the ground along with the sapling, eliminating plastic waste and reducing transplant shock. As the biopot decomposes naturally, it enriches the soil and promotes stronger root development.

Choose Srija Green Galaxy Biopots to grow plants responsibly and contribute to a greener, plastic-free future.
