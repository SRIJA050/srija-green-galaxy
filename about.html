OUR JOURNEY

From a Question to an Innovation

A journey that began with one simple observation and grew into a mission to create sustainable solutions.

19 MARCH 2020

It Started With a Sapling

ZPHS Chintalakunta

“Why are we planting trees while leaving plastic behind in the soil?”

While digging a pit to plant a sapling, we found a black polythene nursery bag buried in the soil.

That simple observation raised an important question about how we could encourage planting while also reducing the waste left behind.

STEP 01

The Question

The discovery of buried plastic during tree planting made us think about the environmental impact of conventional nursery bags.

01
STEP 02

The Problem

We began looking at two connected challenges — persistent plastic waste and agricultural waste that could be discarded or burned.

02
STEP 03

The Discovery

Through experimentation with agricultural residues, groundnut shells emerged as a promising material for developing a sustainable planting product.

03
STEP 04

The Innovation

Groundnut shells were transformed into Biopots, creating a value-added use for agricultural waste and an alternative planting solution.

04
STEP 05

The Journey Continues

Today, Srija Green Galaxy continues to explore sustainable products that give agricultural waste a productive second life.
