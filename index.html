OUR STORY

From a Simple Observation to a Sustainable Innovation

The journey of Srija Green Galaxy began with a simple observation about the impact of plastic on our soil.

On 19 March 2020, while working at ZPHS Chintalakunta, Srija discovered a polythene sapling bag buried in the soil along with a planted sapling.

This moment raised an important question: Can we grow plants without leaving plastic behind?

This idea eventually led to the development of groundnut-shell Biopots — an attempt to replace single-use plastic nursery bags with an environmentally friendly alternative.
